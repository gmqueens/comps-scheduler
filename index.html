Dates

The start date is business day 1 in the procedures. Package deadlines count back from it. The meeting needs to be at least 25 business days earlier.

Minimum is 14, as the procedures require. At 14, each exam is followed by a 4-business-day break and the next reading list goes out the business day after.

A written exam that lands on a Tuesday moves to the Wednesday. Its reading list still goes out 10 business days before it, so the break before it grows by a day. The first exam keeps the start date, so the Candidate gets 11 business days for it.
